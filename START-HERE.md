## In one sentence

Being Human(e) turns grounded observations about ordinary experience, harm, power, tenderness, accountability, access, and repair into accessible public writing.

## If you remember only three things

1. **Field guide first, memoir second, theory underneath.**
2. Compassion without boundaries can enable harm; accountability without humanity can reproduce it.
3. The public guide is the canonical home for published Being Human(e) material; private development belongs in the Atlas.

## Current reality

This is an active public Astro site and writing project. It contains evolving essays, observations, and practical reflection—not clinical advice, a complete moral system, or a claim to speak for every human experience. Personal material should be published deliberately, and factual claims still need appropriate evidence.

## Where it fits

Being Human(e) grounds the wider ecosystem in lived human scale. Its observations can inform Root Sequence concepts, Human(e) Design, Community Infrastructure, Liberation Mass, and creative work, but they do not automatically become software requirements or universal rules.

## What to do next

- **Reader:** open the site or follow the content routes in the [README](README.md).
- **Writer:** begin with one concrete human situation, then ask what care, power, access, uncertainty, and accountability change.
- **Developer:** preserve readability and accessibility; the site exists to carry the guide, not to become the project’s center of gravity.
- **Working with raw/private material:** use the private Being Human(e) Atlas rather than publishing prematurely.
