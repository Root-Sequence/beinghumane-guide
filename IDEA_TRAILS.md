# Being Human(e) — Idea Trails

**Status:** Local relationship index  
**Canonical cross-project map:** [`Root-Sequence/root-sequence/IDEA_TRAILS.md`](https://github.com/Root-Sequence/root-sequence/blob/main/IDEA_TRAILS.md)

Being Human(e) contributes the **ordinary human experience layer** to many Root Sequence Idea Trails.

Its job is not to turn technical or political systems into self-help problems. It asks what systems feel like in lived relationships: what people fear, need, avoid, misunderstand, repair, depend on, consent to, and carry.

---

## Discoverability & Belonging

Human question:

> What stops someone from approaching a community even after they know it exists?

Possible barriers include:

- social anxiety;
- prior exclusion or harm;
- uncertainty about norms;
- disability/access needs;
- cost;
- shame;
- fear of being judged;
- not knowing whether they can come alone;
- not knowing what participation requires;
- exhaustion or limited executive capacity.

Community Infrastructure turns some of these into Discovery Profile / “what to expect” design requirements. Liberation Mass can test them in real gatherings.

## Accessibility, Dependence & Participation

Human question:

---

## Boundary

Being Human(e) should help every Idea Trail stay **recognizably about lived human beings**, but it must not reduce structural problems to individual attitude or interpersonal kindness.

Care matters. So do institutions, material conditions, power, infrastructure, and collective action.
