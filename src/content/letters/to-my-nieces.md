# To My Nieces

This is a partial version of something larger I’ve been building.

I don’t mean for these to be instructions, or definitive conclusions about life. They’re just observations I’ve made over time. Some of it might resonate now, some later, some not at all. That’s okay.

The point is to offer you something to return to, if and when it feels useful — something to think with, not something you have to follow.
