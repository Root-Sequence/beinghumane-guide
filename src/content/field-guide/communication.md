# Communication

- People don’t hear what you mean — they hear what it sounds like to them.  
  Tone, timing, and context can completely change how something lands.

- Being right doesn’t mean being understood.  
  Clarity matters more than correctness in most conversations.

- Silence is also communication.  
  Not responding or delaying still sends a message.
