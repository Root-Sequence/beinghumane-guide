# Project Context — Being Human(e)

## Purpose and scope

Being Human(e) is a public field guide built from grounded observations about ordinary experience, care, harm, fear, shame, grief, love, power, access, accountability, boundaries, and repair.

Its editorial orientation is: **field guide first, memoir second, theory underneath**. It is not clinical advice, a universal moral system, or a software-requirements authority.

## Current reality

This is an active public Astro site and writing repository. It contains evolving, reader-facing material and site infrastructure. Individual pieces may mix observation, interpretation, normative reflection, and sourced facts; those roles should remain legible. Private notes and unresolved personal material do not become public merely because they inform the project.

## Repository structure

`src/content/` owns substantive published material; `src/pages/` and components shape its presentation; `public/` holds static assets; `notes/` holds repository-local supporting work whose publication status must be checked.

## Ecosystem connections

Being Human(e) grounds Root Sequence in ordinary human experience. It may inform Human(e) Design, Community Infrastructure, Liberation Mass, Coherent Computing, and creative projects. Those projects remain responsible for translating observations into their own requirements and evidence rather than citing “humane” as self-justifying authority.

## Working rules

- Begin with a concrete human situation before abstraction.
- Preserve dignity alongside accountability and boundaries.
- Distinguish personal observation, broader factual claim, interpretation, and advice.
- Keep the public writing accessible and let technical structure serve it.
- Route raw, sensitive, and developing material to the private Atlas.

## Update contract

Review when public framing, content architecture, publication boundaries, major themes, or the Atlas-to-guide flow changes.
